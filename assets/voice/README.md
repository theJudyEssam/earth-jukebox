# Recorded intro voice-over (optional)

Drop six MP3 files here and the intro uses them instead of the browser's speech voice:

`intro-1.mp3` … `intro-6.mp3`, one per scene, reading these lines:

1. This is Earth. Our home, seen from space. Everything you see on this globe can also be heard.
2. Oceans cover seventy-one percent of the planet. Here, water sounds like surf, rising and falling like waves.
3. Forests breathe. Over the Amazon, the tallest trees play the highest notes.
4. Storms crackle. Over the Congo, every lightning flash becomes a drum.
5. Ice is changing. Greenland loses hundreds of billions of tonnes of it every year. Change has a sound too.
6. Together, they are the music of a living planet, made from NASA data.

Record them yourself (a phone voice memo works), or generate them with a neural text-to-speech service.
Captions and screen-reader text still come from the script in `src/ui/intro.js`, so keep the wording the same.
